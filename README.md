## About

Historic cities are facing growing climate pressure — rising temperatures, increased flooding, and the risk that adaptation measures unintentionally erode the cultural heritage and social fabric they're meant to protect.

**PAST-FORWARD** develops data-driven adaptation strategies and open digital tools for Historic Urban Landscapes (HULs), combining geospatial analysis, participatory planning, regulatory analysis, and urban landscape design. The project runs **living labs** in Delft, Rotterdam, Xi'an, and Guangzhou, testing and refining strategies together with researchers, policymakers, and local communities.

Funded by **NWO** (Netherlands) and **NSFC** (China), 2026–2030.
